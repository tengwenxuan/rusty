Rusty – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Data accessed

Rusty accesses Google Calendar data (calendars and events) only for calendars that have been explicitly shared with it, and only after the account owner grants access through Google's consent screen.

How data is used

Calendar data is used solely to view, create, and update events at the request of authorized users. It may be processed by an AI model provider only to generate responses to those requests.

Sharing and storage

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as needed to provide the features described above. Access tokens are stored locally on the device running the app.

Rusty's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
